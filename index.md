# Video Flow

Practice controls for any video in Safari: speed, A–B loop, frame stepping and a precise timeline.

## Turn on the extension

1. Open the Video Flow app.
2. In Safari, choose **Safari › Settings › Extensions** and turn on **Video Flow**.
3. Allow it on all websites.
4. Open any page with a video: the Video Flow panel replaces the video's controls.

To turn it off on one website, click the Video Flow icon in the Safari toolbar.
